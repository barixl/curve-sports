Shipping & Delivery Policy

We strive to deliver your products as quickly and safely as possible. Below are the details of our shipping and delivery policy.

1. Processing Time

All orders are processed within 1-2 business days. Orders are not processed, shipped, or delivered on Sundays or national holidays. If we are experiencing a high volume of orders, shipments may be delayed by a few days.

2. Shipping Rates & Delivery Estimates

Shipping charges for your order will be calculated and displayed at checkout. We offer free delivery on orders above ₹499. Standard delivery usually takes 3-7 business days depending on your location. Delivery delays can occasionally occur due to unforeseen circumstances or location constraints.

3. Shipment Confirmation & Order Tracking

You will receive a Shipment Confirmation email containing your tracking number(s) once your order has shipped. The tracking number will be active within 24 hours.

4. Damages & Lost Packages

Curve Sports is not liable for any products damaged or lost during transit. If you received your order damaged, please contact the shipment carrier to file a claim. Please save all packaging materials and damaged goods before filing a claim. If your package is lost, contact us and we will assist you in resolving the issue with the carrier.

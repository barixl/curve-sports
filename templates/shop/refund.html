Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: June 2025  ·  We want you to be 100% satisfied with every purchase.

Cancel before dispatch
7-day return window
Refund in 5–7 days
Easy support process

We stand behind the quality of every product we sell. If something isn't right, we're committed to making it right — quickly and fairly.

1. Order Cancellation

You may cancel your order before it has been dispatched. To cancel:

  • Call us immediately at +91 93121 10022 or +91 98102 27504
  • Or WhatsApp us at +91 93121 10022

Once an order has been dispatched, cancellation is no longer possible. You will need to follow the return process after delivery.

2. Returns

We accept returns within 7 days of delivery only in the following cases:

  • Product received is damaged or defective
  • Wrong product delivered (different from what was ordered)
  • Product is expired

Conditions for a valid return:

  • The product must be unused, sealed, and in its original packaging
  • All tags, labels, and accessories must be intact
  • Proof of purchase (order ID) is required
  • Clear photos of the damage/defect must be shared

To initiate a return, contact us at support@curvesports.com with your order ID and photos within 7 days of delivery.

3. Refunds

Once your return is received and inspected, we will notify you by email or phone within 2 business days. If approved:

  • Refunds are credited to your original payment method (UPI, Card, Net Banking)
  • Processing time: 5–7 business days after approval
  • Bank processing times may add 1–3 additional days depending on your bank

For Cash on Delivery orders, refunds are processed via bank transfer — please share your bank details when contacting us.

4. Non-Returnable Items

For health and safety reasons, the following items cannot be returned or refunded:

  • Opened, unsealed, or partially used nutritional supplements
  • Perishable goods or items with broken seals
  • Gift cards or promotional vouchers
  • Items damaged due to misuse or negligence
  • Products returned after the 7-day window

5. How to Reach Us

For cancellations, returns, or refund queries, contact our support team:

  • Phone/WhatsApp: +91 93121 10022  |  +91 98102 27504
  • Email: support@curvesports.com
  • Hours: Monday – Saturday, 10:00 AM – 7:00 PM IST

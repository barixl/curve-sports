Cancellation & Refund Policy

We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. Please read our cancellation and refund policy carefully to understand your rights and options.

1. Order Cancellation

You may cancel your order at any time before it has been processed and shipped. To cancel, please contact our support team immediately. Once the order has been dispatched, cancellation requests can no longer be processed, and you will need to follow the return process.

2. Returns

We accept returns within 7 days of delivery, provided that the product is damaged, defective, or incorrect. The returned product must be unused, sealed, and in its original packaging with all tags and labels intact. To initiate a return, contact us with your order details and photos of the item.

3. Refunds

Once your return is received and inspected, we will notify you of the approval or rejection of your refund. If approved, the refund will be processed and automatically credited to your original method of payment (e.g., credit card, UPI, bank account) within 5-7 business days.

4. Non-returnable Items

For health and safety reasons, certain items are strictly non-returnable and non-refundable. These include opened or unsealed nutritional supplements, perishable goods, and gift cards.
